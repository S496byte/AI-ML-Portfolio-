02 / Featured project · Artifact 1

Pace — Personal Productivity AI Assistant

A student-built conversational AI prototype designed to help people organize responsibilities and turn overwhelming tasks into manageable next steps.

Conversational AIPrompt designHuman-centered designTestingResponsible AI
The problem

Making daily responsibilities feel more manageable

Balancing study, work, exercise, chores, and personal goals can be difficult. I wanted to explore whether a chatbot could help users prioritize tasks, create realistic routines, and get started without adding unnecessary complexity.

The solution

I created Pace using Mizou, configured its instructions and behavioral boundaries, and evaluated its responses through seven test scenarios. The assistant focuses on small actionable steps, time management, and flexible planning rather than rigid schedules.

Try the Pace chatbot ↗

Process — my five-stage design approach

Empathize

Considered the experience of users who struggle to balance competing responsibilities, motivation, and limited time.

Define

Focused the challenge on prioritization, procrastination, realistic routines, and reducing feelings of overwhelm.

Ideate

Explored a conversational coach that asks clarifying questions and suggests manageable actions.

Prototype

Built the assistant in Mizou with tailored instructions, welcome messaging, and guidelines for its responses.

Test and refine

Used seven scenarios to evaluate helpfulness, relevance, boundaries, and the need to improve instructions.

Responsible design considerations

I considered how a productivity assistant should respond when users raise health-related or emotional concerns. Pace was designed to stay within its productivity-support role and encourage appropriate professional help when needed, rather than pretending to provide medical or therapeutic expertise.

Why this artifact matters

This project provides evidence of my ability to move from a user problem to a working AI prototype, test its behavior, recognize limitations, and refine the design. It is an early example of the practical and responsible approach I want to continue developing as an AI/ML student.

04 / AI agent concept · Artifact 3

Twogether AI — Less Guessing. More Care.

A privacy-first AI agent concept for couples, created for a $100,000 classroom startup challenge.

Agentic AIWorkflow designPrivacy by designProduct strategy
The concept

Turning thoughtful intentions into everyday actions

Couples often manage preferences, plans, and responsibilities across different apps. I explored how an AI agent could help people coordinate shared experiences while keeping individual information under their control.

Twogether proposes two separate private profiles, selective sharing, and an AI planner that can suggest date nights, coordinate calendars, and organize agreed tasks. Optional menstrual wellness tracking remains personal unless a user chooses otherwise.

View investor pitch (PowerPoint) ↗

Agent workflow

Understand a goal → use authorized preferences → check connected tools → propose a plan → request approval for sensitive actions → follow through.

Responsible design

Personal data stays private by default. Users control what the AI may use or share, and bookings, purchases, and sensitive disclosures require approval.

Title

Twogether AI — Privacy-First Shared Wellness & Lifestyle Agent

Introduction

I developed this concept during a classroom AI startup challenge to explore how an agent could solve an everyday coordination problem.

Description

Twogether is a proposed AI companion for couples that organizes preferences, supports shared planning, and offers optional private wellness features.

Objective

Design a realistic agent that uses connected tools and limited autonomy while protecting individual privacy.

Process

I identified the user problem, defined target audiences, designed five agent tasks and an approval workflow, considered risks and integrations, and presented a freemium business model with a proposed $100,000 development budget.

Tools and technologies used

Generative AI for ideation; AI-agent workflow planning; presentation design; concept visuals; proposed calendar, maps, entertainment, and wellness integrations. Integrations were proposed, not implemented.

Value proposition — unique value

The concept combines two private personal profiles, user-controlled sharing, and AI-assisted follow-through on shared plans.

Value proposition — relevance

This artifact demonstrates product ideation, agentic AI design, communication of technical concepts, privacy-aware decision-making, and early business planning.

Title

As an AI/ML student, I am learning to assess not just what I know, but how I approach challenges and respond to feedback. This artifact documents an early step in that process.
